# Ambler All-Events Positioning

Ambler is for **all events**.

The product must not be framed as a birthday app, wedding app, travel app, nightlife app, stag/hen app or festival app.

Those are all valid event templates, but the brand promise is broader:

**Any event becomes a premium storybook.**

## Core positioning

Create an event, invite your people, collect every angle and turn it into a premium storybook with photos, timeline, Route Replay and Story Insights.

## Supported event templates

- Birthday
- Trip
- Festival
- Weekend away
- Night out
- Wedding
- Stag party
- Hen party
- Family gathering
- Graduation
- Work social
- Team event
- Sports trip
- Group holiday
- School/university event
- Private party
- Celebration dinner

## Product copy

Use:
- Any event, one story
- Every angle, one storybook
- Your event, beautifully remembered
- Invite your people. Capture the moments. Relive the story.
- From first photo to final route, Ambler builds the story.

Avoid:
- Built for birthdays
- Built for weddings
- Built for nights out
- Built for trips
- Built for stag and hen parties

## Demo data rule

Demo data must be neutral and removable.

Use:
- Your Weekend Loop
- Demo Storybook
- Shared Event
- The Group Loop

Do not hard-code a real name, city or event category into the product logic.
