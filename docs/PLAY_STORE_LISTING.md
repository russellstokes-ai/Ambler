## Full Description

Ambler turns every event into a polished digital storybook.

Create an event, invite your people, add photos and videos, choose a theme, and watch the best moments come together as a rich visual recap. From birthdays and weddings to trips, festivals, game days, nights out, campus events, brand activations, and family gatherings, Ambler keeps the whole story in one place.

What you can do with Ambler:

- Create events for any occasion
- Invite friends with simple event sharing
- Add selected photos and videos from your device
- Capture optional event routes for map moments
- Pick from expressive visual themes
- View your event as an animated storybook
- Revisit galleries, routes, highlights, captions, and reactions
- Share storybooks with the people who were there

Ambler is built for shared memories, not endless feeds. Each event becomes a keepsake with its own mood, movement, and rhythm, so the recap feels as good as the moment did.

Why people use Ambler:

- Everyone's best shots belong in one place
- Event recaps should feel premium, not scattered
- Trips, hikes, match days, parties, and celebrations deserve more than a folder
- Routes, places, captions, and reactions make memories feel alive
- Storybooks are easy to revisit and share

Privacy matters. Media is user selected, location is optional and event-only, and shared content is built around the people invited to the event.

Capture it together. Build the story. Relive the whole thing.

## Category Recommendation

Primary recommendation: Photography

Alternate category: Social

Rationale: Ambler's core value is creating visual event recaps from user-selected media. Social features support the media experience, but the main store positioning should emphasize photo and video storybooks.

## Content Rating Notes

Expected rating: Teen / 13+

Play Console questionnaire considerations:

- User-generated content: Yes
- Users can share photos, videos, captions, comments, and reactions: Yes
- Users can interact with other users in shared events: Yes
- Location sharing: Optional, event-only, visible in event/storybook context
- Mature content: Not part of intended app experience
- Violence: Not part of intended app experience
- Gambling: No
- Drugs, alcohol, or tobacco promotion: No app-level promotion, but user event content may vary
- Profanity: Not app-authored, but user captions/comments may vary
- Moderation and reporting: Add clear controls before broad public release

## Target Audience

Target audience: 13+

Ambler is not directed to children under 13. Store listing, privacy policy, and onboarding should align with a 13+ audience.
