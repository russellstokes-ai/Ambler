# Ambler Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Ambler helps people create shared event storybooks from photos, videos, route moments, captions, and event details. This Privacy Policy explains what information Ambler collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

This policy is written for public hosting, such as GitHub Pages or another simple web URL.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Email address
- Display name
- Profile avatar
- Authentication provider account identifier

Account sign-in may be provided by Expo Auth Session, Google, Facebook, Apple, or Supabase Auth. These providers may process sign-in information under their own privacy policies.

### Photos and Videos

Ambler lets you choose photos and videos from your device for an event. Selected media may be uploaded and stored so the event storybook can be built, displayed, shared with invited participants, and restored across devices.

Ambler is designed to use the Android system photo picker through Expo Image Picker where available. This lets you choose specific items instead of giving broad library access.

### Location Information

Location use is optional. If you choose to enable route capture for an event, Ambler may collect approximate or precise location points during that event. Location is used to show event routes, stops, map moments, and place-based storybook pages.

Ambler does not need location in the background unless a future release clearly asks for that permission and explains why. Location capture should be event-only, user-controlled, and shown before permission is requested.

### Event and Storybook Information

We may collect the event details you create or join, including:

- Event name, type, date, and theme
- Invited participants and event membership
- Captions, reactions, comments, and selected highlights
- Generated storybook pages, routes, galleries, and share links

### Device and Technical Information

We may collect basic technical information needed to operate the app, protect accounts, debug errors, and improve reliability. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and diagnostics. If analytics or crash reporting tools are added, this policy should be updated before release.

## How We Use Information

Ambler uses collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Let users create, join, and manage events
- Store selected photos, videos, captions, routes, and storybooks
- Show shared event content to invited participants
- Support account recovery and authentication
- Improve app performance, reliability, and security
- Respond to support requests
- Enforce the Terms of Service and protect users from abuse

## Storage and Processing

Ambler is built to use Supabase as a data processor for authentication, database records, and media storage. Supabase may store account data, event records, selected media, location records, and related app data on Ambler's behalf.

Some app data may also be stored locally on your device for offline access, faster loading, or session continuity. Local data can usually be removed by signing out, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

## Sharing of Information

Ambler does not sell personal information.

We may share information in these limited ways:

- With invited event participants, when you add content to a shared event
- With people who can access a storybook or share link that you or another event participant sends
- With service providers that help operate the app, such as Supabase, Expo, Google, Facebook, Apple, hosting providers, and future crash or analytics providers
- When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to continued protection of user information

## User Content and Visibility

Photos, videos, captions, comments, routes, and storybooks may be visible to other participants in the same event. Users should only upload content they have the right to share and should be thoughtful about other people who appear in media.

If a storybook is shared through a public or semi-public link, anyone with that link may be able to view it until access is revoked or the content is deleted.

## Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain accounts, support shared events, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may delete events, media, routes, storybooks, or accounts where deletion controls are available. Backup copies or logs may remain for a limited period before they are removed through normal retention processes.

## Data Security

Ambler uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. Data transmitted between the app and backend services should be encrypted in transit using HTTPS or equivalent transport security.

No internet service can guarantee perfect security. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access the personal information associated with your account
- Correct inaccurate account information
- Delete your account or selected content
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Export a copy of your information where technically available
- Withdraw optional permissions, such as location or media selection

You can manage device permissions in your operating system settings. Turning off location may limit route and map features. Removing media access may limit photo and video selection.

## Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting Ambler support. Deletion requests should include the account email address and a clear request to delete the account.

Some shared event content may involve multiple participants. When one user deletes their account or content, Ambler will make reasonable efforts to remove that user's personal information while preserving event records that other participants are entitled to keep, unless deletion is legally required.

Production support contact: to be published before release

## Children's Privacy

Ambler is intended for users age 13 and older. Ambler does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Users under the age of majority in their region should use Ambler with permission from a parent or guardian.

## International Processing

Information may be processed and stored in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws may differ by location, but Ambler will take reasonable steps to protect information as described in this policy.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date will be revised when the policy is updated. Continued use of Ambler after changes means the updated policy applies.
